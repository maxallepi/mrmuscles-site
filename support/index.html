Support

Something broken?
Say so.

Something broken, or something you want added? Write in. One person reads everything, so a clear message with the details below gets a faster answer.

What to send

  • Bugs: what happened, what you expected, your device and Android version.
  • Data deletion: ask directly and name the Play Games account you used.
  • Ad issues: note the ad, roughly when it appeared, and what you were doing.
  • Feature requests: describe the problem first, then the fix you have in mind.

Email support@chickenflap.win

Expect a reply within a few days. It is one person, so weekends and busy weeks happen.

Chicken Flap documents

Everything the Play listing points to, plus the policies behind the game.

  • Privacy policy — what the game stores, what Google collects for ads and Play Games.
  • Terms of service — the rules for using the game.
  • FAQ — leaderboards, Play Games profile visibility, achievements, checkpoints.
  • Google account permissions — revoke Play Games access at any time.

Data deletion

Chicken Flap keeps your scores and unlocks on your device, and — if you sign in — in your own Google Play Games saved-games profile. To delete it:

  • Uninstalling the app clears all local scores, coins, skins and unlocks.
  • Play Games cloud data can be cleared from the Play Games app: Settings → Delete Play Games account & data.
  • For a deletion request handled by us directly, email support@chickenflap.win from the address you used on the device.
