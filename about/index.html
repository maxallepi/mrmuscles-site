About

Small studio.
Small games. Done properly.

Mr.Muscles Inc. is an independent game studio making small arcade games for Android. It exists because most mobile games are built to hold attention rather than reward it, and there is room for the opposite. One developer, one game at a time.

Games start small: one mechanic, understood completely, then tuned until it holds up under repeated play. Everything is built and tested on real Android devices, including the cheap ones. If a feature does not make the core loop better, it does not ship.

Every release follows the same rules. No pay-to-win, no forced sign-in, no purchases that decide who wins. Games work offline where the design allows it, and ads stay out of the way of play.

The plan is to keep doing this: a small catalogue of arcade games that are easy to start and hard to put down. Chicken Flap is the first. The rest will follow when they are ready, not before.

Chicken Flap

Chicken Flap is a one-tap endless arcade game: tap to flap, thread the pipes, see how far you get. Four difficulties, seven worlds and eleven skins to unlock, plus thirty-five Play Games achievements if you want them. Free, ad-supported, no in-app purchases.
